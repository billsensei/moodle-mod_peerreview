# Peer review: how to review your classmates

1. **Open the activity** on your phone: go to the course and tap the peer review activity.
2. Under **Reviews to do** there is a card for each classmate you should review. Tap a card (**Start review**).
3. **Fill in the form.** On a rubric, tap one box in each row. Add a short comment that says what was good and what could be better.
4. Tap **Submit review**. If you need more time, tap **Save draft** and come back later (**Continue review**).
5. Changed your mind? Tap the card again (**Edit review**). You can change your review until the activity closes.

When all your cards say **Submitted**, you are done.

**Your feedback:** when your teacher releases it, the section **Feedback I received** appears under your cards. It shows your grade and your classmates' comments. Reviewers are usually shown as "Anonymous".

Be fair, be specific, be kind.

**In the Moodle app:** the activity opens inside the app. You can see your reviews to do, give a score and a comment, and read the feedback you received. If your teacher uses a rubric or marking guide, the button opens the review in your browser instead.
